## Critique
- Costs: does it survive realistic fees and slippage?
- Out-of-sample: was it tested on data not used to find it?
- Multiple testing: how many variants did they try before this one?
- Bias risks: look-ahead, survivorship, data snooping
- Transferability: would it work on NQ and ES, today?
